About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a fast, straightforward tool built to help you capture your thoughts instantly. It ensures your most important ideas are safely recorded without getting in your way.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your idea into the input field on the home page.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button or press Enter to save it.
  3. View and manage your saved notes in the list below the form.

Features

  • Instant note capture
  • Clean and accessible HTML5 interface
  • Keyboard shortcut support
